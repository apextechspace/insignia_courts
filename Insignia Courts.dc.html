The Tea Station

Within the residence, you’ll find a Japanese-inspired tea station and a curated décor collection, with select pieces available for purchase.

It sits where it makes sense to sit: between the entrance and the lounge, available when available. Take a cup up to your room, or stay with it a while. Nobody will hurry you.

Room & Tea Corner Selection

A regular selection of tea bags will be provided in the rooms; black, green, chamomile, etc., while our signature herbal ones will be at the general tea corner.

In your room

Select rooms carry a coffee and tea corner; the executive rooms and suites include a fitted kitchenette.

Ask, and it is arranged

Tea can be sent up with breakfast, or set out for a meeting. Speak to the team the evening before.

Long stays & corporate

For weeks,
not nights

A serviced residence answers a longer stay better than a hotel does. Kitchenettes in the executive rooms and suites, laundry on site, errands handled, and a rate that reflects the length of the booking rather than the calendar.
